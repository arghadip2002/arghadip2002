## 👨‍💻 About Me

I'm **Arghadip Biswas**, a Computer Science graduate currently pursuing a **Master of Technology in Intelligent Computing**.

My primary ambition is to become a **Machine Learning Engineer**, developing intelligent systems that bridge research and real-world applications.

I'm particularly interested in:

- 🤖 **Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning** — Developing intelligent, data-driven systems.
- 🧠 **Deep Learning & Computer Vision** — Neural networks, visual understanding and image analysis.
- 🏥 **Medical Imaging** — AI-driven medical image segmentation, classification and computational healthcare.
- 🚀 **Robotics** — Intelligent perception, localization and autonomous systems.
- ✨ **Generative AI & Agentic AI** — LLMs, AI agents and intelligent automation.
- 🔗 **RAG & LLM Applications** — Retrieval-Augmented Generation and knowledge-driven AI systems.

I enjoy exploring research papers, experimenting with architectures and transforming ideas into practical engineering solutions.

## 🔬 Research & Projects

### 🧠 Brain Tumor Classification & Segmentation

**Custom Residual Inception Module U-Net Approach**

- Developed a deep learning approach integrating custom Residual Inception modules with U-Net.
- Focused on brain tumor classification and segmentation using MRI images.
- Explored feature extraction, multi-scale learning and semantic segmentation.

**Domain:** Medical Imaging | Deep Learning | CNN | U-Net

### 🔬 Brain Cell Instance Segmentation — Currently Working

**SAM-Based Foundation Models for Medical Imaging**

- Exploring Segment Anything Model (SAM) and MicroSAM for brain cell instance segmentation.
- Investigating parameter-efficient fine-tuning approaches such as LoRA.
- Studying resource-efficient adaptation of foundation models for histopathological images.
- Working with neuronal and glial cell segmentation.

**Domain:** Medical Imaging | Foundation Models | SAM | MicroSAM | LoRA

### 🚁 Robotics & Visual Localization

Interested in intelligent robotic perception, visual navigation and localization, including hierarchical localization and 6-DoF pose estimation.

**Domain:** Robotics | Computer Vision | Visual Localization | Deep Learning
